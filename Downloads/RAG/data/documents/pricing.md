# Pricing and Plans

Northwind Analytics is sold as a monthly or annual subscription. All prices are in US dollars and exclude applicable taxes.

## Plans

The Starter plan costs $49 per month and includes 5 seats, 10,000 API calls per month, and 10 GB of storage.

The Growth plan costs $199 per month and includes 25 seats, 100,000 API calls per month, and 100 GB of storage.

The Enterprise plan uses custom pricing negotiated per contract. Enterprise includes unlimited seats, a negotiated API quota, dedicated infrastructure options, and a designated solutions engineer.

## Additional seats

On the Growth plan, additional seats beyond the included 25 cost $12 per seat per month.

## Annual billing

Annual billing receives a 20 percent discount compared with paying monthly for twelve months. Annual plans are invoiced upfront for the full term.

Usage overage charges are not eligible for the annual discount. Only the base subscription fee is discounted.

## Usage overage

API calls beyond the plan quota are billed at $0.002 per call. Overage is metered continuously and billed monthly in arrears, regardless of whether the subscription itself is billed monthly or annually.

## Free trial

New customers receive a 14 day free trial that does not require a credit card. At the end of the trial the workspace is paused unless a paid plan is selected.

## Price changes

Northwind gives at least 60 days written notice before any change to subscription pricing. Notice is sent to the billing contact on file.
