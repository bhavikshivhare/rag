# Data Retention and Deletion

## Active accounts

Customer data is retained for as long as the subscription is active. There is no automatic purging of active workspace data.

## After cancellation

When a subscription is cancelled, the workspace is placed in a read-only state for 30 days. Customer data is permanently deleted 30 days after the cancellation date. During the 30 day window the customer can export data or reactivate the subscription.

## Backups

Encrypted backups are retained for 35 days on a rolling basis. Backups are stored in the same region as the workspace. Deleted customer data ages out of backups within the 35 day retention period and is not restored on request.

## Audit logs

Audit logs are retained for 12 months on the Enterprise plan. Starter and Growth workspaces retain audit logs for 90 days.

## Deletion requests

Customers can submit a right-to-erasure request from the workspace settings page. Verified erasure requests are completed within 14 days, except where a legal hold or statutory retention obligation applies.

## Export formats

Data can be exported as CSV or JSON at any time. Export files are available for download for 7 days before they are removed from temporary storage.
