# Support and Service Levels

## Support tiers

Standard support is included with Starter and Growth plans. Premium support costs 15 percent of the annual contract value. Enterprise plans include 24/7 support at no additional charge.

## Coverage hours

Standard support is staffed 9:00 to 18:00 Eastern Time on weekdays, excluding public holidays.

Premium support is staffed 24 hours a day on weekdays and 8 hours on weekends.

Enterprise support is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays.

## Response targets

For Standard support, the first response target is 8 business hours for a P1 issue, 1 business day for P2, and 3 business days for P3.

For Premium support, the first response target is 1 hour for P1, 4 hours for P2, and 1 business day for P3.

For Enterprise support, the first response target is 30 minutes for P1, 2 hours for P2, and 8 business hours for P3.

## Severity definitions

P1 means the production service is unavailable or customer data loss has occurred. P2 means the service is significantly degraded but still usable. P3 means a question, documentation request, or minor defect.

## Escalation

A ticket automatically escalates to the on-call engineer when 50 percent of its response target has elapsed without a first response. Customers can also request escalation from the support portal.

## Uptime commitment

Northwind commits to 99.9 percent monthly uptime for the API and the web application. If monthly uptime falls below this commitment, the customer receives a service credit of 10 percent of the monthly fee for each additional 0.5 percent below 99.9 percent.

## Status communication

Incidents are published on the public status page at status.northwindanalytics.com. Post-incident reviews are shared with affected Enterprise customers within 5 business days.
