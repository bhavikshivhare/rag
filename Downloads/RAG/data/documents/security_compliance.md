# Security and Compliance

## Certifications

Northwind Analytics holds SOC 2 Type II certification, audited annually by an independent third party. The full SOC 2 report is available to customers and prospects under a non-disclosure agreement.

Northwind is also certified to ISO/IEC 27001. The certificate scope covers the platform, the hosted service, and supporting infrastructure.

## Encryption

Customer data is encrypted at rest using AES-256. Data in transit is protected with TLS 1.3. Encryption keys are managed in a dedicated key management service and rotated every 90 days.

## Authentication

Single sign-on is supported through SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect. SCIM 2.0 user provisioning is available on the Enterprise plan only.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all administrator accounts and optional for standard users.

## Penetration testing

A third party penetration test is performed every year. An executive summary of the most recent test is available to customers on request. Critical findings must be remediated within 30 days.

## Data residency

Customers can choose between two hosting regions: the United States (Virginia) and the European Union (Frankfurt). Region selection is made at workspace creation and cannot be changed afterwards without a migration project.

## Audit logs

Enterprise workspaces have access to audit logs covering authentication, permission changes, exports, and API key usage. Audit logs are retained for 12 months.
