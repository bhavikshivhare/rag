# Refund Policy

## Refund window

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of their initial purchase. The 30 day window starts on the date the first invoice is paid.

For annual plans, a pro-rated refund is also available within 30 days of an annual renewal date.

## How to request a refund

Refund requests must be submitted through the support portal or by email to billing@northwindanalytics.com. Each request must include the invoice ID and the workspace ID.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days. Northwind cannot issue refunds to a different card or bank account.

## Exclusions

The following are not refundable: add-on services, professional services engagements, overage charges for API usage, and custom development work.

## Downgrades and cancellations

Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date and do not produce a partial refund for the current period. Cancellations stop future billing but do not refund the current billing period unless the request falls inside the 30 day refund window.

## Chargebacks

Customers are asked to contact billing support before filing a chargeback. Accounts with an open chargeback may have API access suspended until the dispute is resolved.
